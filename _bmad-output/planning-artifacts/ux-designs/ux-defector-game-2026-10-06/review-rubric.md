## Overall verdict

The HUD contract is strong. Every persistent bar, its rect, its colour and its behaviour agree across DESIGN.md, EXPERIENCE.md, the decision log, the GDD and the epics. Convoy rules (y 760–920, 100 HP, 5/2/10 damage, solid hulls), the boss bar maths (300 HP, markers at 66.7% / 16.7%, per F8), the interruption cap (≤ 50 chars, max 2 per level, 45 chars/s, `max(2.0, chars/45 + 1.2)`), the typewriter speed and the Esc rules all match. There are **no blockers**. The gaps are at the edges: the non-HUD screens (title, level select, pause, end, story scene) have IA entries but no layout. The hint slot breaks the spine's own "no HUD below y 432" rule. Vale and `system` have no speaker styling. The Final Choice and the cut-off each leave one behaviour undefined.

### Should-fix (13)

- **S1. The hint slot contradicts "no HUD in the player zone".** DESIGN.md:94 puts hints at y 560, and DESIGN.md:95 says the player zone runs from y 432 to 1080 with "**No HUD**". DESIGN.md:138 says "keep the player zone (below y 432) free of HUD". EXPERIENCE.md:98 calls y 560 "the boundary", but the boundary is y 432. The epics carry the same clash: UX-DR1 at epics.md:207 against UX-DR4 at epics.md:210. A dev or test that enforces UX-DR1 would reject the hint pill. *Fix:* keep y 560 (decision #8), but reword DESIGN.md:94/95/138 and EXPERIENCE.md:98 to say "No persistent HUD below y 432; the temporary hint pill (y 534–586) and the paused overlays (story panel, Final Choice, retry, pause menu) are the only exceptions." Change "on the boundary" to "just inside the top of the player zone, above the ally band (y 620)".

- **S2. The menu screens have IA but no layout or visual spec.** EXPERIENCE.md:22–29 lists the Title (PLAY · LEVEL SELECT · QUIT), Level Select (three cards, locked cards greyed), the Pause menu ("centred menu panel `PAUSED`", EXPERIENCE.md:84) and the End screen. DESIGN.md Layout (87–99) and Components (120–132) give none of them a rect, button size, card size or locked-state style. Only the 120px logo and 48px heading sizes exist (DESIGN.md:80). Stories 4.1, 4.3, 4.4 and 7.7 are told to "follow its Layout & Spacing table" (epics.md:17), and the table is silent on all four screens. *Fix:* add one block of Layout rows. For example: Title — logo centred at y 300, a vertical button stack of 320×80 buttons with 24px gaps starting at y 560. Level Select — `SELECT LEVEL` heading 48px at y 200; three 480×240 cards in a centred row with 48px gaps at y 420, showing the level name 28px and number; locked cards at 40% opacity with a `muted` border and a `LOCKED` label (not colour alone); BACK button at y 800. Pause — a 560×400 `surfaceAlt` panel centred over `dim`, `PAUSED` 48px, then three stacked 320×80 buttons. End — title 120px at y 320, closing line 30px at y 480, "Thanks for playing" 28px `muted` at y 560, BACK TO TITLE 320×80 at y 720. Add a `levelCard` and a `menuPanel` entry to the frontmatter `components`.

- **S3. The story scene (opening) has no state or layout.** EXPERIENCE.md:25 names it. The GDD (gdd.md:350) and Story 5.5 define it as a separate `story.tscn` with a static space background, a visible cursor and click-to-advance. The State Patterns table (EXPERIENCE.md:79–87) has no row for it. Is the HUD hidden? Is the background dimmed? Is the panel at the same 410,300 rect? *Fix:* add a State Patterns row: "Story scene | No HUD | Visible arrow | No dim (static background is already dark) | Same centred story panel rect and behaviour; Esc does nothing (no level running)". Also say whether Esc opens the pause menu here. Story 4.4 only defines Esc "when a level is running".

- **S4. Vale (and `system`) have no speaker styling.** The speaker tokens cover only `isolde`, `tarek` and `ryn` (DESIGN.md:26–28, 72). Story 5.1 validates speaker ids `vale, isolde, tarek, ryn, system` (epics.md:1073). Vale speaks before each Isolde refusal (F17, epics.md:1560; EXPERIENCE.md:145), and the GDD makes Vale's portrait optional. With no accent colour and no rule for a portrait-less panel, the dev has to guess. *Fix:* add a `vale` token (for example `coalition` `#22E6FF`, or a distinct hex) and a `system` token (for example `muted`). In DESIGN.md Components, add a "no portrait" variant of the story panel and the interruption box: the text block widens to fill the portrait slot (story panel text 1036px), and the accent edge stays.

- **S5. The Final Choice leaves the story-panel cue and the click handling undefined.** DESIGN.md:97 and EXPERIENCE.md:83 keep the story panel visible with the buttons below it. EXPERIENCE.md:91 says "Click anywhere advances paused dialogue". The mock (key-hud.html:378) shows the `click to continue ▸` cue and the typing caret *while* the buttons are up. That tells the player to click anywhere when they must click a button. *Fix:* in the EXPERIENCE.md:74 Final Choice row, state: "Once the buttons appear, the cue and caret hide, and clicks outside the two buttons are ignored. Only a button click continues." Add "except during the Final Choice" to EXPERIENCE.md:91. Update the mock frame 3.

- **S6. The interruption box never says whether it types, but the cut-off depends on it.** EXPERIENCE.md:66 says the box "slides in" and stays for `max(2.0, chars/45 + 1.2)` s. EXPERIENCE.md:67 says the cut-off "stops mid-word". Story 5.4 (epics.md:1172) and the architecture say the *typewriter* stops at `cut_off_at`. Without a typing rule, the dev can't tell whether interruption text types at 45 chars/s or appears instantly, or when the stay timer starts. *Fix:* add to the Interruption box row: "Text types at 45 chars/s (same as the story panel); the stay timer starts when the box opens; a `cut_off_at` line stops typing at that character."

- **S7. The accessibility floor contradicts the interruption text size.** EXPERIENCE.md:117 says "dialogue is 30px". Interruption dialogue is 24px (DESIGN.md:32, 80, 90, 122; log #16). A dev who follows the floor would set 30px and overflow the 96px box. *Fix:* reword EXPERIENCE.md:117 to "No text smaller than 20px at 1080p; story dialogue is 30px and interruption dialogue 24px."

- **S8. The story panel has no length cap and no overflow rule.** The panel is 1100×300 with 32px padding and an 812px text block with 30px text (DESIGN.md:96, 123). After the name line that leaves about 4 lines. Nothing caps a story line, and no data test checks it. The interruption box has both (≤ 50 chars, epics.md:1124). Story 5.7 writes the script with no limit. *Fix:* add "≤ 160 characters per story line, autowrap, max 4 lines; enforced by `test_data_files.gd`" to DESIGN.md:96 and Components, and note it for Stories 5.1 and 5.7. [Tune the number once the font is in-engine.]

- **S9. "Move" and "fire" are two hints but there is one pill slot.** EXPERIENCE.md:58 defines `MOVE THE MOUSE TO FLY` and `[HOLD LMB] FIRE` as separate hints. Flow 1 step 3 (EXPERIENCE.md:130) shows both appearing together. DESIGN.md:94/99 defines a single pill at y 560. Story 5.8 has one `move_fire` hint id (epics.md:1263). The spines don't say whether this is two stacked pills, one combined pill, or two in sequence, or which action dismisses it. *Fix:* pick one and state it. For example: "`move_fire` is one pill reading `MOVE THE MOUSE TO FLY · [HOLD LMB] FIRE`, dismissed on the first laser fire (or after 5 s)". Or: "two pills, the second 64px below the first at y 624". Note that y 624 overlaps the ally band, so the first option is cleaner.

- **S10. The shield bubble has no size and no behaviour.** DESIGN.md:131 says only "`coalition` ring around the ship at 40% alpha". It has no frontmatter `components` entry (DESIGN.md:37–48), no diameter and no stroke width. The EXPERIENCE.md Component Patterns table (63–75) has no row for it: no appear or expire animation, and no warning before the 4.0 s expiry (Story 3.4). The E/Q hints are taught but the bubble isn't specified. *Fix:* add `shieldBubble: 'coalition ring, 96px diameter, 3px stroke, 40% alpha fill + glow'` to the frontmatter. Add an EXPERIENCE row: "Shield bubble | Pops in over 0.15 s on activation; blinks at 4 Hz during the last 1.0 s; fades over 0.15 s at 4.0 s; pressing E while active does nothing (no denied flash, per Story 3.4)."

- **S11. The boss-bar / interruption-box clearance is hand-waved, and one rule exists only in DESIGN.** The boss bar spans x 600–1320 at y 40–60. The interruption box spans x 32–592 at y 24–120, which leaves an 8px horizontal gap. DESIGN.md:91 says "The boss bar sits lower than the interruption box's text line", which is false (the bar is at y 40–60; the text lines are at about y 58–114). DESIGN.md:91 also says "interruptions are suppressed during the boss intro". That behaviour isn't in EXPERIENCE.md, isn't in any story, and isn't in the data test. *Fix:* delete the "sits lower" clause. Move the rule into the EXPERIENCE.md Interruption box row as "No combat interruptions during the boss wave (L3's only interruption is in W3)", and add it to the Story 5.2 data test (no `combat` comms trigger in a `boss` wave). Alternatively, narrow the boss bar to 640 (x 640–1280) for a 48px gap.

- **S12. The mock link is broken in both spines.** DESIGN.md:134 and EXPERIENCE.md:151 link `mockups/key-hud.html`. There is no `mockups/` folder; the file is `.working/key-hud.html`. *Fix:* move the mock to `mockups/key-hud.html` (preferred, since `.working/` reads as scratch), or change both links to `.working/key-hud.html`. Also link it inline from DESIGN.md Layout & Spacing (line 85), the section it mostly illustrates.

- **S13. "No briefing replay on retry" is promised but not specified anywhere a story reads.** Flow 2 step 3 (EXPERIENCE.md:140) commits that retry skips the briefing. The IA (EXPERIENCE.md:26–28) and the State Patterns don't say it. No story implements it: Story 5.5 only skips the *opening*, Story 5.3 plays `intro_sequence` "when the level starts", and Story 3.2 only sets `is_retry`. *Fix:* add to the IA Retry bullet: "Retry and Restart Level skip the level briefing (`intro_sequence`) and go straight to wave 1 (`GameState.is_retry`)". Raise it against Story 5.3 (epics.md:1148): "skip `intro_sequence` when `is_retry`".

- **N12. The hearts row doesn't say how it grows.** GDD Difficulty Curve names "+1 heart" as the first tuning lever. *Fix:* add to DESIGN.md:92 "hearts row is right-anchored at x 1888 and grows leftward".

- **N16. The config-error state is missing.** The title screen's config-error label (Story 4.1) isn't in the State Patterns. *Fix:* one line: "Config error: plain `danger` label centred on the title, PLAY disabled."
- **N17. The boss-bar marker count needs a citation.** The GDD (gdd.md:292) says "three segment markers"; the spine has 2 markers on one bar (DESIGN.md:41, 125). Epics F8 reconciles them, but the spine doesn't cite it. *Fix:* add "(2 markers = 3 segments, per epics F8)" to DESIGN.md:125.
