# Epic 8 Context: Polish and Release

<!-- Generated from planning artifacts. Regenerate with compile-epic-context if planning docs change. -->

## Goal

Turn the finished, playable campaign into a shippable v1.0. That means real CC0 audio and a coherent neon art pass in place of the placeholders, measured proof that the build meets its frame-time, launch-time and size targets, three clean start-to-finish playthroughs with every story beat firing plus a short playtest, and a zipped 64-bit Windows build published as a GitHub release that anyone can download, unzip and play. The audio and art passes (8.1, 8.2) are SHOULD and the first to be cut. Performance, finishability and release (8.3–8.5) are MUST. Stories 8.1–8.4 are built. Their look, sound and hands-on performance checks, and 8.4's three playthroughs and playtest, are owed to the operator.

## Stories

- Story 8.1: Make the Game Sound Right

## Requirements & Constraints

- **Performance (measured, 8.3):** a steady 60 FPS (under 16.6 ms per frame) at 1920×1080 on the dev laptop's integrated GPU, in Level 3 Wave 3 and in the Flagship's DESPERATE phase. Cold launch to the title in under 5 s. Build under 200 MB. All passed with wide margins (worst p99 about 7.7 ms, launch about 2 s, release exe about 118 MB). No pooling was needed and Forward+ is kept. Still owed: hands-on play with the F1 overlay, and a true cold launch after a reboot.
- **Stability (8.4):** three full playthroughs on a release build from a fresh save, with no crash or soft-lock. Every beat must fire in every run: the opening, Isolde's interruptions, the L1 surrender prompt (a SURRENDER / REFUSE choice), Tarek's fall, Ryn's report, the L3 Final Choice, Isolde's final line, the council epilogue and the end screen. They are checked against the INFO `beat` lines in `%APPDATA%\DEFECTOR\logs\godot.log` with the audit tool (below). The results go in the record sheet `implementation-artifacts/playtest-8-4-record.md`.
- **Playtest (8.4):** 3–5 people, no verbal help. Record whether they understood the controls by Level 1 Wave 2, attempts per level, total playtime (target 10–15 min, 1–3 attempts per level), and their answers to "Was the shooting fun on its own?" and "Which story moment do you remember?". Make any retune in data only (`tuning.json`) and log it in the decision log. Tests check playtest-tuned values only by range, so a retune needs no test edits. The ally fire interval (1.0 s) is provisional. Recheck it against the 10–15% ally kill share using the `Kills: ... ally M (P%)` line (`dev_ally`, and per attempt in the campaign).
- **Open questions the playthroughs and playtest should settle:** does the Level 1 convoy (100 HP) survive real play; do Level 2's Bastions shield the Needles; is the throne-world arc visible; does the boss-death sweep cause a one-frame spike; is the loudness right (non-tier SFX play at 0 dB and nothing limits summed clipping); is it acceptable that music carries over into the title, Level Select and retry-to-title (only the final level clear fades it).
- **Release (8.5):** a 64-bit Windows zip with no installer. `DEFECTOR-v1.0-windows.zip` holds the exe, a `README.txt` with the controls, and the credits. The repo `README.md` covers the premise, the controls, how to download and run, and the credits. Smoke-test the zip in a clean folder: it launches in under 5 s, creates the save in `%APPDATA%\DEFECTOR\`, and plays through Level 1. Publish with `gh release create v1.0 ... --notes-file RELEASE_NOTES.md`, **only after explicit user confirmation**. The same applies to any push or other `gh` write.
- **The README must match the current game rules:** Play always starts Level 1 through the opening, and Level Select jumps ahead. Energy is never saved, only unlocks persist. Controls: the ship sits on the mouse cursor; hold LMB for the laser, RMB fires a missile, E raises the Shield (50 energy), Q fires the Barrage (100 energy); LMB advances comms and picks a choice; Esc pauses; F11 toggles fullscreen.
- **Public-repo hygiene:** no secrets, emails or machine-specific paths anywhere, export presets included (relative paths only). Never commit `game/export/`, `*.zip` or `game/.godot/`. Every file under `game/assets/` must be in `game/assets/CREDITS.md` with its source and licence (`CC0 1.0` or `Project-owned`). A data test enforces this.

## Technical Decisions

- **Audio (built):** `Audio.play_sfx(id, ...)` uses a registry of 20 preloaded SFX ids, each its own file: Kenney CC0 combat sounds, plus the generated comms and alarm cues and the recorded `comms_static`, kept as they are. Two voice pools (12 combat, 2 comms) so combat never cuts off comms. Explosion pitch and volume per size tier come from `tuning.feedback.tiers` (pitch small 1.2 > medium 1.0 > large 0.8). Two CC0 looping tracks, `combat` and `boss`, at `audio.music_volume_db` -10 dB, started by wave `music` actions (the level-root `music` key is never read). `music: stop` silences Tarek's fall. Clearing the last level fades the music before the epilogue or end screen. No ducking, no menu or end-screen track. Retune loudness in `tuning.json`.
- **Rendering and art (built):** Forward+ with `hdr_2d` and `WorldEnvironment` glow (`render.glow`). Glow sprites also use an additive `CanvasItemMaterial`, so a Compatibility fallback stays a one-setting change. Anything that must not bloom keeps every channel at or below the 0.55 threshold, and backgrounds keep every channel at 127 or below. Kenney sprites are recoloured by `tools/import_cc0_art.py` and sized through `SpriteFit` from the existing size data. The Flagship hull and the three portraits are project-owned SVGs. Projectiles, orbs, explosions, the shield and the HUD stay drawn in code. Re-run the headless `--import` after adding or changing assets.
- **Theme and font (built):** one shared `defector_theme.tres` with Kenney Future as the default font. Dialogue text keeps the engine font through base-less FontVariations, because Kenney Future breaks the 4-line story panel. No per-node overrides beyond the comms portrait frame.
- **Performance tooling:** `-- --bench=N` (sandbox) and `-- --level-bench=busiest_wave|desperate` (debug export, `DEFECTOR.console.exe`) pass only if avg, p99 and max frame time are all under the `debug.bench` limits. `tools/perf_check.py size|launch` takes its limits from `debug.release_check`. Release builds buffer the file log, so `launch` reads stdout. Re-run the benches after any visual change. If instancing ever causes an overrun, add pooling inside `Spawner` only. No per-frame logging.
- **Finishability tooling (built, 8.4):** the `[INFO][beat]` lines (story scene `Story '<id>'`, comms `Choice '<id>' picked in '<sequence>'`, end screen `End screen: campaign finished ...`, WaveDirector intro/comms/story-event/outro lines) and the boot `Build: release|debug` line are a contract with `tools/beat_audit.py`, so keep their text exact; a format test checks them. `beat_audit.py --latest 3` (or explicit log paths, `--allow-debug`) passes a run when it is a release build, the save was fresh, every required beat fired in order (flag-off beats are skipped), the end screen was reached, and there are no error, crash or skipped-Tarek's-fall lines. It also reports attempts per level, time to the end screen, choices and ally kill share. Release logs flush only on exit, so audit after the game quits. The required-beat manifest lives in the tool, tied to `game/data` by a test. No autoplay exists; the runs are the operator's.
- **Export:** the Windows Desktop preset is committed with an embedded PCK, product name "DEFECTOR", the non-resource filter `data/*.json` (without it the JSON is missing from the build) and the exclude filter `addons/gut/*, tests/*`. Story 8.5 set the application icon to `res://assets/icon/defector_icon.svg` (also `project.godot`'s window icon) and the single version source `application/config/version="1.0"`; `RELEASE_NOTES.md` exists at the repo root. Both release tools exist: `tools/package_release.py` builds `DEFECTOR-v<version>-windows.zip` (`DEFECTOR.exe`, `release/README.txt` as `README.txt`, and `CREDITS.txt` = `CREDITS.md` plus the Godot licence notices from `release/GODOT_COPYRIGHT.txt`, generated by `tools/godot_copyright.gd`), and `tools/repo_hygiene.py` scans the tracked files and archives for secrets, real emails and machine paths. For a CLI export, run the headless `--import` first, then `--export-release "Windows Desktop" export/windows/DEFECTOR.exe`. Godot 4.7.2 export templates must be installed.

## UX & Interaction Patterns

- **Palette tokens:** `background` #05070F, `coalition` #22E6FF (allies, convoy, player), `enemy` #FF2BD6 (all Sovereignty ships and **all** enemy shots), `energy` #FFD23F, `hazard` #7A6E62 with `hazardRim` #C9D8E8, `danger` #FF1F4B, `text` #E8F4FF, `muted` #93A7C2. Speaker accents (Isolde, Tarek, Ryn) appear only in the comms UI.
- **Typography:** at 1080p, 20 px HUD labels, 28 px body and comms, 30 px names, 48 px headings, 120 px title. Nothing below 20 px. Labels, buttons and names are UPPERCASE; dialogue is sentence case.
- **Depth and layout:** depth comes from glow, not shadows. The persistent HUD stays in the top 120 px band. The throne-world arc hangs from the top edge, on Level 3 and the end screen only.

## Cross-Story Dependencies

- 8.4's playthroughs and 8.5's smoke test need the final release build. Any retune from 8.4 is data-only and lands before 8.5's release. Re-run the benches if a retune or fix changes visuals or entity counts.
- 8.5 depends on a complete CREDITS.md (8.1, 8.2), the installed 4.7.2 export templates, and the operator's sign-off on the provisional audio, art, performance and finishability checks.
- The campaign is already finishable end to end from Epic 7. This epic changes assets, tuning, logging and packaging, not game flow. Fixes for the open questions above should stay small and data-driven.
